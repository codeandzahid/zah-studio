# Zack D. Films style — short guide

The style the Green Penny finance Shorts use: smooth 3D simulation,
not flat motion graphics. A money tree grows, coins rain in, a $100
bill sits in glass while the groceries it buys disappear — the idea is
shown in 3D, not told with flat bars.

## What Zack D. Films uses

- **Blender** is the main tool. Their animators build the 3D scenes,
  characters and simulations in Blender. Their hiring page lists
  Blender skill as a major plus.
- **Cascadeur** for character movement (physics-assisted animation).
- **After Effects** for compositing and finishing.
- **EmberGen** for smoke, fire and explosion effects.
- **Flamenco** (Blender's render manager) to run renders fast.
- Some artists on the team also use **Unreal Engine** (fast real-time
  rendering, MetaHuman characters) and **Houdini** (big simulations).

It is a team of 50+ animators, not one person — that is how they
publish 1-2 high-quality 3D shorts a day.

## Rules for making videos in this style

- Show the idea in 3D. Never use flat bars or flat motion graphics.
- Numbers on screen must be clean overlays added in editing, never
  generated inside the animation — AI generation garbles digits.
- One clear visual metaphor per video (a growing tree, an emptying
  cart, a snowball of coins).
- Smooth camera moves, high clarity, slightly exaggerated realism.
